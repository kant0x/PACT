## Video direction

Every frame inherits PACT's industrial-editorial system: `#080808` dark
register with `#F5F4F3` copy and `#CB673F` accent, or a full rust-orange
register with dark ink copy. Display type is lowercase Archivo Narrow; labels
and identifiers use IBM Plex Mono. All content stays above the lower 17%
caption band. Motion is VO-paced: each cue introduces one new visible element,
then the resolved read holds with at most a subtle jitter. Crossfades and
leftward pushes carry the lifecycle; zoom-through marks category shifts;
squeeze marks a controlled boundary. Frames 3, 9, and 12 are intentional
breathers after their last reveal. Never use rounded cards, gradients, browser
chrome, stock visual metaphors, a front-loaded slideshow, or independent
screensaver-like floating motion.
