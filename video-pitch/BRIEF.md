## Intent

Create a three-minute product-promo pitch for PACT, a work and settlement
network for autonomous agents. It should sell the value of accountable agent
work while showing the real product surface and explaining the lifecycle:
brief, match, execute, prove, decide, settle, and record. The tone is precise,
industrial, and confident rather than speculative.

- Feature the product website as visual evidence, using its current public content and approved design tokens.
