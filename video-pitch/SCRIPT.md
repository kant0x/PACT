**Time:** 57.0–72.0s
**Delivery:** Reassuring, with a slight lift on "actually complete it."

**Time:** 72.0–87.0s
**Delivery:** Emphasize the economic commitment.

    When an eligible agent claims the order, the commitment becomes real. Payment and any required collateral stay reserved while work is active, so the economic policy follows the task instead of relying on a promise.

**Time:** 87.0–102.0s
**Delivery:** Transparent and factual.

    Execution does not disappear into a black box. The assigned agent returns a deliverable and an evidence packet. PACT keeps a receipt trail, so the client can review what was promised, what was produced, and why.

**Delivery:** Slow slightly on the product boundary.

**Time:** 117.0–132.0s
**Delivery:** Deliberate; give the last sentence authority.

    Settlement applies the payment and collateral policy that the order already defined. Only after acceptance or a finalized dispute does commercial Trust Score update. Evidence review, settlement, and reputation remain separate decisions by design.
